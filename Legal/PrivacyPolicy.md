# PlantPal Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: March 12, 2026**

Chad Newbry LLC ("we," "us," or "our") built PlantPal as a privacy-first app. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

## Summary

**PlantPal does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.** All your plant data stays on your device and your personal iCloud account.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect analytics or usage data
- We do not collect personal information (name, email, location)
- We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs
- We do not use third-party analytics services
- We do not fingerprint your device

## Data Stored on Your Device

PlantPal stores the following data locally on your device:

- Plant names, species, photos, and notes you enter
- Care schedules (watering, fertilizing, repotting, light tracking)
- Care event history and calendar data
- App preferences and settings

This data is stored using Apple's Core Data framework and never leaves your device except via iCloud Sync (described below).

## iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud on your device, PlantPal syncs your plant data across your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is stored in **your private iCloud database** and is:

- Encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple
- Accessible only to you through your Apple ID
- Not accessible to us or any third party
- Subject to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

You can disable iCloud sync for PlantPal in your device's iCloud settings at any time.

## AI Plant Health Features

PlantPal's AI plant health analysis runs **entirely on your device** using Apple's on-device machine learning frameworks. No plant photos or health data are sent to external servers for processing.

## Notifications

PlantPal uses local notifications to remind you about plant care schedules. These notifications are generated entirely on your device. No data is sent to external servers to schedule or deliver notifications.

## Apple Watch & Widgets

PlantPal's Apple Watch app and Home Screen widgets display your plant care data locally. No data is transmitted externally for these features.

## Siri Integration

When you use Siri with PlantPal, your voice interactions are handled by Apple according to Apple's Siri Privacy Policy. PlantPal provides plant care data to Siri locally through App Intents.

## Data Export & Deletion

- **Export:** You can export all your plant data as a CSV file from Settings > Data Management > Export Plant Data.
- **Deletion:** You can delete all your data at any time from Settings > Data Management > Delete All Data. This permanently removes all plant data from your device. To remove data from iCloud, disable iCloud sync or delete the data from your iCloud storage settings.

## Children's Privacy

PlantPal does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
